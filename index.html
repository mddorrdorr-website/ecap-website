About ECAP

Bridging Eastern and Western business practice for Africa.

Our programmes combine the best of Eastern and Western business practices while addressing the unique opportunities and challenges facing the African continent.

At ECAP we believe Africa's future lies in the hands of its people. By investing in world-class professional development, we help individuals and organisations unlock their full potential, drive innovation and create lasting impact.
